# Red Bull New Product Launch – Marketing Project

## Project Overview

Group academic project developed as part of the Marketing course during my Bachelor's degree.

The project involved an analysis of Red Bull's market positioning, competitive environment and target customers, followed by the development of a new product concept and a strategy for its market launch.

## Objectives

- Analyse Red Bull's current market positioning and brand strategy
- Evaluate the competitive landscape and relevant market trends
- Identify potential customer needs and market opportunities
- Develop a new product concept consistent with the Red Bull brand
- Define the target market and positioning of the new product
- Develop a marketing and go-to-market strategy for the product launch
