# Red Bull New Product Launch – Marketing Project

Group academic project developed as part of a **Marketing** course during my Bachelor's degree.

The project analyses **Red Bull's market positioning, competitive environment and target customers**, then develops a new product concept and a go-to-market strategy for its launch.

---

## Project Overview

The project combines company and market analysis with new product development.

The work focused on understanding Red Bull's current positioning and identifying a market opportunity that could support the launch of a new product while remaining consistent with the brand.

The final proposal covers:

- market and brand analysis;
- competitive analysis;
- target customer identification;
- product concept development;
- target-market definition;
- product positioning;
- go-to-market strategy.

---

## Objectives

- Analyse Red Bull's current market positioning and brand strategy
- Evaluate the competitive landscape and relevant market trends
- Identify potential customer needs and market opportunities
- Develop a new product concept consistent with the Red Bull brand
- Define the target market and positioning of the proposed product
- Develop a marketing and go-to-market strategy for the launch
